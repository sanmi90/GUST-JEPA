Generated by `scripts/session29/manifold_diagnostics.py`. test_b, full-context rollouts (`z_full`), d = 64. Query = rollout latent at frame impact+H; reference manifold = per-frame TRAIN encoded cloud at the modal frame impact+H (verbatim `drift_ce1.py` convention). Both diagnostics use NO covariance inverse, so there is no shrinkage knob to dismiss.

## Method

- kNN distance: mean Euclidean distance to the k = 10 nearest TRAIN cloud points, normalised by the cloud scale (RMS distance of TRAIN points to their centroid).
- local-PCA residual: fit a rank-5 PCA on the query's k = 20 nearest TRAIN neighbours, report the norm of the query's component orthogonal to that local tangent subspace, same normalisation.
- Both rotation-invariant and free of any covariance regularisation.
- Significance: paired per-encounter (reconstructive - predictive) with the case-clustered block bootstrap + one-sided sign test from `stats_lib` (`case_permutation_p` deliberately NOT used; B6 lesson).
