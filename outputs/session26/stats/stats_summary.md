## 1c. Predictive vs the conditioning floor (wake enstrophy, frame-matched c-only KRR, H=16)

Floor reproduced exactly: c-only KRR(RBF) test_b R^2 = **0.173** (recipe alpha=0.1, gamma=0.03,

| comparison | enc-mean dErr (floor - JEPA) | case-clustered 95% CI | case Wilcoxon | verdict |
|------------|------------------------------|------------------------|---------------|---------|
| forecast vs floor | +7.6 | [-15.5, 31.4] includes 0 | p=0.35, 4/10 | **not above floor at case level** |
| representational vs floor | +19.1 | [-2.0, 45.9] grazes 0 | p=0.097, 7/10 | positive, marginal at case level |

The forecast wake R^2 (0.449) exceeds the floor R^2 (0.173) as a variance-weighted aggregate,
but the equal-weight per-encounter improvement over the floor is NOT established at the case
level (only 4 of 10 cases, CI includes 0). The representational closure R^2 (0.754) is well above
the floor (0.173); its per-encounter improvement is positive in 7 of 10 cases but the
case-clustered interval grazes zero. Conclusion: the margin over the conditioning floor is
carried by the high-variance encounters that dominate R^2, not by a uniform per-case improvement.
This is why the paper re-anchors on representation + mechanism rather than the marginal
forecast-over-floor margin (Track 6).
