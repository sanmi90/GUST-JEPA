\section{The transferred-forecasting ledger}
\label{app:rex_ledger}

The \DirectFC{} of \S\ref{sec:methods_rex} was built by auditing the
TiRex forecasting line \citep{tirex,tirex2} ingredient by ingredient against this
data. Adopted: the direct multi-horizon head, the quantile output, the
per-window instance normalisation and the arcsinh transform, each of which
survived ablation on validation. Not supported by validation for this
data: future-known
covariate conditioning; the oracle $(G, D, Y)$ arm degrades the forecast
in all three seeds (\S\ref{sec:res_forecast}), overfitting held-out
parameter combinations at the present case count. Rejected: capped-weight
robustness in both forms tried, a saturating output nonlinearity that
exploded during the gust transient and a loss-side weight cap that
down-weighted exactly the transients the encounter is about. Deferred:
the variate mixer and test-time adaptation, neither needed at this scale.
The backbone bake-off at matched budget ties a hand-written
exponential-gated cell \citep{xlstm} with the plain LSTM within seed
noise; the plain cell at width $512$ with nine quantiles is used
throughout.
