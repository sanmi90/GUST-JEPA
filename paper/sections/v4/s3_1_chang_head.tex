\subsubsection{The near-body lift-element head}
\label{sec:methods_chang}

\rb{A}{S31-01}{Full construction of a head the paper itself calls a lift-sharpening refinement, not the primary state, with two equations and its own quality gate. Its result already sits in appendix A, so the method should join it.}The third supervision head targets a physically constructed near-body
observable built on the force-element decomposition of \citet{chang1992},
in the form popularised for vortex-dominated
flows by \citet{menon2021}. An auxiliary
potential $\phi_L$ is defined by the exterior boundary-value problem
\begin{equation}
  \nabla^2 \phi_L = 0 \ \ \text{outside the body}, \qquad
  -\,\mathbf{n} \cdot \nabla \phi_L = \mathbf{n} \cdot \mathbf{e}_L
  \ \ \text{on the body}, \qquad
  \phi_L \to 0 \ \ \text{far away},
  \label{eq:phiL}
\end{equation}
with $\mathbf{e}_L = (-\sin\alpha, \cos\alpha)$ the lift direction at
$\alpha = 14^\circ$. Equation~\eqref{eq:phiL} is discretised once on the
$192 \times 96$ cache grid with a staircase immersed Neumann condition on
the solid-adjacent cell faces and a homogeneous Dirichlet far field, and
solved directly (figure~\ref{fig:phi}); the interior Laplacian residual of the stored solution is
$6.4 \times 10^{-13}$ in the maximum norm. The lift-element density is
then the pointwise field
\begin{equation}
  e(\mathbf{x}, t) = \omega_z \left(-v\,\partial_x \phi_L
  + u\,\partial_y \phi_L\right),
  \label{eq:lift_element}
\end{equation}
evaluated from the raw mid-plane velocity and vorticity with the stored
sign convention verified empirically (the cache stores
$\partial u/\partial y - \partial v/\partial x$, so the standard
$\omega_z$ is its negation). Because the cache window truncates the
domain, the full-domain integral of \eqref{eq:lift_element} does not track
the lift; the band-restricted field does, and the head therefore
supervises $e$ inside a feathered near-body band $w(\mathbf{x}) =
\max\{0,\, 1 - \delta(\mathbf{x})/0.3c\}$, with $\delta$ the Euclidean
distance to the body and its adjacent cells. The banded field $w\,e$,
scaled by a fixed constant chosen so its 99th percentile magnitude is
order one, is compressed to an $80$-dimensional target by exactly the
wake-observable construction: $64$ sign-preserving patch energies (an
$8 \times 4$ grid of $\log(1 + \mathrm{relu}(\pm x)^2)$ pooled energies)
concatenated with a $16$-bin Hann-windowed radial power spectrum. The two
$80$-dimensional targets are byte-matched in capacity, and the wake and
near-body heads share one network class, so the conditioning comparison
of \S\ref{sec:res_construction} isolates the supervision signal, not the
head architecture. A precompute quality gate requires the median lagged
correlation between the band-integrated element and the stored lift over
the gust-bearing training encounters (lag search $|\ell| \le 25$ frames)
to exceed $0.4$; the achieved value is \NbQcMedianCorr. The principled
weighting is not interchangeable with a plain vorticity-magnitude proxy:
the patch-block cosine between the two targets is \NbProxyCosine{} on the
comparison sample. The auxiliary potential and its supervision band are shown in
figure~\ref{fig:phi} in appendix~\ref{app:decode_figures}.\re
