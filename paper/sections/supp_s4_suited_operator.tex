\section{The suited-operator forecast merit}
\label{s:suited_merit}

Before the shared-operator recomputation, the families table scored each
state's forecast merit under the operator numerically stable on it: a
matched autoregressive transformer on the pooled states of the controlled
matrix and a matched residual U-Net on the reference latents, on which the
transformer diverges. Those values, at horizon eight, are retained here
because the choice of operator is itself informative: the no-wake
predictive state that collapses under the shared \DirectFC{}
($\XmeritShEightJepaNowake$ at the same horizon) posts $\XmeritJepaNowake$
under its suited transformer, and the reference recipes reorder. The
shared-operator column of the main text (table~\ref{tab:closure}) is the
primary comparison; this table documents the superseded protocol.
