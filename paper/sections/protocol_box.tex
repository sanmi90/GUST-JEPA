\noindent\fbox{\parbox{\dimexpr\linewidth-2\fboxsep-2\fboxrule\relax}{%
\textbf{Evaluation protocol (frozen before the held-out evaluation).}
\emph{Rollout.} One convention for every family, figure, and table: the predictor is
initialised with the full pre-impact context, all encoded frames up to and including the
per-encounter impact frame (attending to at most $T=32$ latent history frames), and runs
autoregressively with no teacher forcing thereafter. Horizons are counted from the impact
frame; we report $H\in\{4,8,16,32\}$ with $H=16$ primary. Single-frame (Markov) seeding is
not used.
\emph{Estimation.} Held-out $R^2 = 1-\mathrm{SSE}/\mathrm{SST}$ with SST about the held-out
split's own mean, over per-encounter pairs at impact${}+H$. Probes are fitted on training
latents only, with five-fold case-level cross-validation on the readout; three probe classes
(linear ridge, primary; kernel ridge with RBF kernel; small MLP) are reported for both the
representational endpoint (encoded held-out fields) and the forecast endpoint (full-context
rollout).
\emph{Uncertainty.} Bootstrap resampling unit is the encounter ($n=2000$); every wake claim
additionally carries a case-clustered interval (cluster unit: case); the ten-test family
(five observables, two endpoints) is Holm-corrected.
\emph{Primary endpoint.} Representational wake-enstrophy $R^2$ at $H=16$ on the
\TestSplit{} split, fixed in the archived analysis plan (decision-log entries
D130/D165, repository commit \CommitHash) before the held-out evaluation.
\emph{Selection.} Headline numbers are quoted at fixed $d=64$ as seed mean $\pm$ sd; the full
family$\times d\times$observable$\times$endpoint$\times$probe matrix is in the appendix.
\emph{Decoders.} Every visualisation decoder shares one recipe and budget; each family's
operating point is the checkpoint with maximum validation SSIM under the same rule. Decoded
fields support visualisation and the labelled decode-ceiling comparison only: the
spectral-amplitude loss term is phase-blind, so no quantitative localization claim rests on
decoded fields.
